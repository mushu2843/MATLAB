# Experiment 1 : 
LEVEL 1 : Simulation of Analog (AM, FM) and Digital (ASK, FSK, PSK) Modulation Techniques 

To generate a message signal and a carrier signal in MATLAB, and to study analog modulation (Amplitude Modulation and Frequency Modulation) and digital modulation (ASK, FSK, BPSK) through simulation and observation of the resulting waveforms.

LEVEL 2 : BER Performance Analysis of BPSK and QPSK over an AWGN Channel 

To simulate a digital communication link using BPSK and QPSK modulation over an Additive White Gaussian Noise (AWGN) channel in MATLAB, and to plot Bit Error Rate (BER) versus Eb/No, comparing simulated performance against the theoretical BPSK curve.
